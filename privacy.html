Privacy Policy

ATELIER Studio · last updated 4 October 2026

ATELIER Studio ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool that its owner uses to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

Who uses the app

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not used to access anyone else's Google or YouTube account.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's own sign-in screen, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, to the owner's channels (youtube.upload);
  • read basic information about those channels and their videos: the channel's name and ID, and a video's processing status, privacy status and public view, like and comment counts (youtube.readonly).

The app does not access viewers' personal information, comments' authors, subscribers' identities, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data.

How the information is used and stored

The information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how they are doing. Access tokens and the channel information above are stored on the owner's own computer and are not sent anywhere else. The app does not sell, share, or transfer any of it to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer removes everything it stored.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
