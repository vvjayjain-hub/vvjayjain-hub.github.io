ATELIER Studio

A private video studio for a small set of YouTube channels.

ATELIER Studio is a personal, self-hosted tool. Its owner uses it to prepare videos for their own YouTube channels and to upload them to those channels after reviewing each one. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and does not collect information from anyone else.

When the owner connects a channel, the tool asks Google for permission to:

  • upload videos to that channel (YouTube scope youtube.upload), and
  • read basic information about the channel and its own videos, such as the channel name and public view counts (YouTube scope youtube.readonly).

The channels it publishes to include Homelab AI, How It Actually Works and Spilled Tea Stories.
